Health Insurance Policy - Gold Plan
Policy Number Format: POL-H-XXXXX

Section 1: Coverage and Limits
- Maximum claim amount per incident: Rs. 2,00,000
- Annual deductible: Rs. 10,000
- Pre-existing conditions are NOT covered for the first 24 months

Section 2: Claim Processing Rules
- Claims below Rs. 50,000: Auto-approved if documents are complete
- Claims between Rs. 50,000 and Rs. 2,00,000: Require human review
- Claims above Rs. 2,00,000: Must be escalated to senior adjuster
- Emergency hospitalization claims are processed within 24 hours

Section 3: Exclusions
- Cosmetic procedures
- Pre-existing conditions (first 24 months)
- Self-inflicted injuries
- Injuries from adventure sports
