Motor Insurance Policy - Comprehensive Plan
Policy Number Format: POL-M-XXXXX

Section 1: Coverage and Limits
- Maximum claim amount per incident: Rs. 5,00,000
- Third-party liability: Unlimited as per Motor Vehicles Act
- Own damage deductible: Rs. 5,000

Section 2: Claim Processing Rules
- Claims below Rs. 25,000: Auto-approved if documents complete
- Claims between Rs. 25,000 and Rs. 1,00,000: Human review required
- Claims above Rs. 1,00,000: Escalate to senior adjuster
- Accident claims require police FIR copy

Section 3: Exclusions
- Driving under influence of alcohol/drugs
- Driving without valid license
- Mechanical failure due to poor maintenance
- Racing or speed testing
- Commercial use of private vehicle
